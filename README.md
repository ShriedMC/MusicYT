# 🎵 MusicYT
**MusicYT puts your music back in your hands. Enjoy free, ad-free listening straight from your local drive compeletely offline.**

Skip the subscriptions and the mid-song interruptions. Find, download, and organize virtually any track from YouTube into custom playlists through a sleek, lightweight player.

# ✨ Key Features
- Zero Ads, Zero Buffering: Play your music locally without sponsored breaks or slowdowns.

- True Offline Mode: Download your tracks once and keep the vibes going anywhere.

- Built-in Search: Discover and grab new tracks directly inside the app.

- Custom Playlists: Build, sort, and manage your mixes effortlessly.

- Roadmap Ahead: More audio tools, better themes

# ⚙️ Prerequisites
Internet Connection: Needed only when searching and downloading new tracks.
Web Browser: Modern browser required (Google Chrome strongly recommended).
